1. Who we are

Vyro — Creator OS is a creator workspace project maintained by its developer. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact piyushkumar158742@gmail.com. This contact address is intended for privacy and service-related correspondence.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to information processed through the Vyro website and its workspace features, including video planning, pipeline stages, tasks, brand/deal notes, calendar events, personalization settings, account sync, and the optional YouTube channel connection. It does not govern websites or services operated by other organizations, even when linked from Vyro.

This document is intended to be clear about current functionality, not to claim that every possible feature is already implemented. If a feature changes the kinds of data collected or the way data is used, this policy should be updated before or when that feature is introduced.

3. Information we process

3.1 Information you enter

You may enter video titles, descriptions, notes, workflow stages, task details, dates, calendar events, brand names, deal amounts, and display preferences. These details may contain personal or business information if you choose to enter it. Please avoid storing passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in notes.

3.2 Account information

If you choose Google sign-in or email/password authentication, Firebase Authentication may process account identifiers and authentication data such as your email address, display name, profile image, and user ID. Password authentication is handled by Firebase; Vyro's interface does not need to read or store your password in the workspace data. Google sign-in is governed in part by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

3.3 YouTube information

If you explicitly connect a YouTube channel, Vyro requests read-only YouTube access and may retrieve public/channel-authorized information such as channel name, channel identifier, thumbnail, subscriber count when visible, total views, video count, recent upload titles, publication dates, thumbnails, video identifiers, and view counts. The current importer is designed to import up to the 200 most recent uploads into the workspace. YouTube may not provide hidden metrics or every field for every video.

3.4 Technical and diagnostic information

Your browser, hosting provider, Firebase, Google, or other infrastructure may process ordinary technical data needed to deliver and secure the Service, such as IP address, request timestamps, browser/device information, error events, and authentication logs. We do not promise that Vyro itself operates a separate analytics or advertising-tracking system.

3.5 Information we do not intentionally request

Vyro is not designed to collect precise location, contacts, private messages, payment-card details, or unrelated Google Drive/Gmail content. Do not grant permissions that appear broader than the functionality you intend to use. The YouTube connection is intended to be read-only.

4. Workspace content and storage

Vyro can work without an account. In that mode, workspace data is stored in your browser's local storage on that device. Local browser data may be lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, change browsers, or reset the workspace. Other people who can access your device or browser profile may be able to view locally stored workspace information.

If you sign in, Vyro may sync the workspace state to a Firebase Firestore document associated with your Firebase user ID. The application is designed so each signed-in user accesses their own workspace document. Configuration and security rules must remain correctly set up for that separation to be effective; no internet service can guarantee perfect security.

When you connect YouTube, imported channel/video information becomes part of your workspace. Disconnecting YouTube is intended to remove imported YouTube video entries from the workspace and stop the app's active connection; it does not delete the videos from YouTube or revoke any Google-side authorization unless you separately revoke it in your Google Account permissions.

5. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • display and organize your creator workspace;

  • authenticate you and, if enabled, sync workspace data between sessions or devices;
  • connect to YouTube only after you initiate the connection, and show the channel/video information requested by the app;
  • maintain the Service, troubleshoot errors, protect accounts and data, and prevent abuse;
  • respond to your support or privacy requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce the Service's terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the YouTube data accessed through this connection to build advertising profiles or to serve targeted advertisements. Information should not be used for a purpose materially different from those described here without appropriate notice and, where required, consent.

9. Retention and deletion

Local workspace data remains in your browser until you delete it, reset the workspace, or clear site data. To remove local data, use the workspace reset function where available or clear site data for this website in your browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

For cloud-synced data, signing out does not necessarily delete the stored cloud workspace. Contact piyushkumar158742@gmail.com to request deletion of a cloud workspace or account-related information. We may need to verify the request before acting. We aim to handle valid requests within a reasonable period and as required by law. Copies may remain temporarily in backups, logs, or provider systems according to their retention practices or where retention is legally required.

Disconnecting YouTube in Vyro is not the same as revoking the app's permission in Google. To revoke access, review the third-party connections in your Google Account security settings. Once permission is revoked, Vyro should no longer be able to make new authorized YouTube requests using that authorization.

10. Security

We use reasonable design and configuration measures intended to protect workspace information, including Firebase authentication and user-scoped Firestore access rules. You should keep your devices and accounts secure, use a unique password, avoid sharing signed-in devices, and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

No browser application, database, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure. The Service may be hosted on third-party infrastructure and may be under active development. We cannot guarantee that data will never be accessed, lost, altered, or disclosed due to every possible attack, configuration error, provider incident, or device compromise. Do not use Vyro as the sole repository for critical records; keep backups of important work.
